QR → buyer self-complete → agent approval → REX + Realworks

A buyer scans a property's QR code, completes an offer on their phone, and the listing agent confirms it before anything is written to your systems. On approval the offer is pushed to REX and a pre-filled Realworks contract is generated — no double data entry.

  1. Buyer scans the listing QR code and lands on the offer form, pre-filled with the property.
  2. Buyer enters their details and terms and submits — nothing is binding.
  3. The offer lands in the agent's approval queue (Emily's screen).
  4. Agent reviews, can adjust, then approves — watch the Integration Console push to REX & Realworks.
